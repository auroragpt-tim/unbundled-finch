TAB. I — Services

What we prepare.

Unbundled Finch handles the document infrastructure so you can focus on the work that requires your legal expertise. Select only the services you need.

SVC. 01
Table of Authorities

Precise compilation and formatting of all legal citations within a brief, motion, or appellate document. Cases, statutes, regulations, and secondary authorities organized to jurisdiction-specific court standards.

SVC. 02
eBinder Assembly

Complete digital case binders assembled and organized for litigation, arbitration, or appellate proceedings. Documents indexed, tabbed, and structured for immediate reference and navigation.

SVC. 03
Exhibit Hyperlinking

PDF exhibits linked, cross-referenced, and made navigable for electronic filing and review. Internal hyperlinks embedded so attorneys and reviewers can move through documents efficiently without manual page-searching.

SVC. 04
Pleading Formatting

Pleadings formatted to meet court-specific requirements: caption, margins, line spacing, numbering, pagination, and signature block. Jurisdictional compliance reviewed before delivery.

SVC. 05
Document Processing

General-purpose processing for legal documents requiring clean formatting, structural organization, or format conversion. Intake-to-delivery workflow managed through the client portal with a reference number assigned at submission.

SVC. 06
Record Extraction

Organized extraction and compilation of records from medical, administrative, or legal file sets. Documents identified, indexed, and compiled into a structured reference format ready for legal review or submission.

SVC. 07
PDF Remediation

Scanned, flattened, or unstructured PDFs corrected and optimized: text recognition, layer cleanup, accessibility formatting, and e-filing preparation. Delivered as a clean, compliant document file.

SVC. 08
Quality Control

Final-stage review of processed documents before filing or submission. Formatting verification, citation accuracy, index review, and structural check against court or client specifications before release.

TAB. III — Process

How it works.

A structured intake-to-delivery workflow built for legal professionals. Every project assigned a reference number. Every document reviewed before delivery.

01
Submit Your Request

Use the client portal to submit your project details, document type, jurisdiction, and turnaround requirements. First-time clients can submit a general inquiry directly through the contact form. Every project receives a reference number at intake.

02
Secure Document Upload

Upload your files through the encrypted client portal. Documents are received, logged, and confirmed. Your files are handled with professional discretion at every stage and never shared with third parties.

03
Processing and Review

Your documents are processed according to your specifications. Before delivery, every file is reviewed against formatting standards, court requirements, and client instructions. We do not release work that has not been checked.

04
Structured Delivery

Completed documents are delivered through the portal in the format you specified — organized, formatted, and ready for filing, submission, or distribution. Reference documentation included.
