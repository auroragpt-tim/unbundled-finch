How to read these results

The charts present published survey responses, not the author's judgments or voting recommendations. A 100% bar may represent only a few ratings; the total number varies by question and respondent group. Blank bars mean the selected group has no plotted responses for that question, not an unfavorable evaluation. The visualizations currently focus on the February–September 2025 questionnaire, with distinct presiding-judge survey results where available.

These are voluntary survey findings, not judicial voting records, and they do not replace the Arizona Commission on Judicial Performance Review's full assessment. The site is not affiliated with the Arizona judiciary or an election office.

Sources and scope

Based on the official 2026 Arizona Judicial Performance Review reports. The dataset covers judges appearing on the Maricopa County judicial-retention portion of the 2026 general-election ballot, where applicable.

It does not cover candidates in partisan races, ballot propositions, or every elected judicial office. Information is presented for independent voter evaluation.
